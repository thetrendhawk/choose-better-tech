## Methodology and testing transparency

Small purposive sample, one complimentary account, separate August and September procedures, and distinction between archived bytes, current previews and historical notes are appropriate. The partial category rubric is not a whole-product rating and must not appear as one. Current speed/reliability score is qualitative; no standardized latency comparison exists. Exact browser version/OS, paid tier, backend credits and current export properties remain unverified where not recorded. No new credits spent by reviewer.

## Source quality and visible sources

Official policies establish representations only. The Shopify listing independently confirms listing existence, not integration performance. SaaSayer is competing coverage, not the basis for contested findings. First-hand source/output comparisons provide the main original contribution. The manuscript must visibly link official policies/pricing and the public sanitized test records. Private source/output media must not be embedded while rights remain unresolved.

## Duplicate intent and reader value

The product-specific fidelity and factual-copy experiment is distinct from the broader ecommerce-tools pillar plan. Practical value is explaining when attractive assets can misrepresent the photographed item and which checks a seller must retain. A text-only manuscript can communicate this without licensing unresolved media or filling missing benchmarks with guesses.
