## Purpose

Build a focused Choose Better Tech coverage pillar for tools that help ecommerce sellers and small businesses create product imagery and other commercial visual assets with AI. This is narrower than the existing general AI Productivity roadmap: the buyer, workflow, and evaluation standard are specifically tied to online selling and product presentation.

## Initial Audience

- Ecommerce brands and small businesses
- Shopify, Amazon, and other marketplace sellers
- Agencies, marketers, and content creators producing product visuals

## Founding Evaluation

Rewarx Studio AI is the pillar's first hands-on product evaluation. The company describes the service as turning product photos into commercial-quality visuals for online stores, ads, marketplaces, and social media.

The evaluation must test practical ecommerce outcomes, including:

- faithfulness to the source product;
- visual quality and commercial usability;
- workflow speed and ease of correction;
- controls, consistency, export options, and credit consumption;
- pricing and value for realistic seller workloads;
- privacy, rights, and material usage restrictions;
- failure modes that could misrepresent a product to buyers.

## Editorial Independence

Choose Better Tech joined the Rewarx Studio AI affiliate program through Awin and received complimentary testing credits. Those facts must be disclosed in any resulting coverage. They do not authorize a favorable verdict, ranking, tracked link, call to action, or publication.

The editorial assessment must be based on reproducible testing. Affiliate economics and complimentary access are commercial context, not evaluation criteria.

## Initial Content Opportunities

No comparison set, ranking, title, or publication sequence is final until the first evaluation establishes what the category can reliably deliver.
